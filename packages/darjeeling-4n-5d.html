Overview

Day 1: Arrive in Kalimpong and explore Deolo Hill, Durpin Monastery, Cactus Nursery, and Morgan House, enjoying beautiful Himalayan views and peaceful surroundings before your overnight stay in Kalimpong. Day 2: After breakfast, drive to Darjeeling, check in to your hotel, and spend the evening exploring Chowrasta and Mall Road, enjoying local cafés, shopping, and the charming hill-town atmosphere. Day 3: Begin with the spectacular Tiger Hill sunrise, followed by Ghoom Monastery and Batasia Loop, then visit the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute, Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park, Peace Pagoda, and Happy Valley Tea Estate before returning to your hotel. Day 4: After breakfast, drive to Kurseong, a peaceful hill town surrounded by tea gardens and misty mountains, and visit Eagle’s Craig, Dow Hill, and nearby tea gardens before enjoying a relaxing evening and overnight stay. Day 5: After breakfast, check out and proceed towards Bagdogra Airport/NJP Railway Station or your next destination, concluding your memorable Kalimpong, Darjeeling, and Kurseong Himalayan journey.

Day by day

The itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Arrival at NJP/IXB – Transfer to Kalimpong

    Arrive at NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport (IXB) and meet our representative. Begin your scenic drive towards Kalimpong, enjoying the beautiful Himalayan landscapes, green valleys and winding mountain roads. On arrival, check in to the hotel and relax. Later, enjoy leisure time exploring the local surroundings and Kalimpong Market. Overnight stay in Kalimpong.

    NJP/IXB PickupScenic Himalayan DriveTeesta River ViewsMountain LandscapesKalimpong MarketPhotographyHotel Check-in

    BreakfastStandard Room
  2. Day 2

    Kalimpong Sightseeing – Transfer to Darjeeling

    After breakfast, proceed for Kalimpong sightseeing. Visit Deolo Hill, offering panoramic views of the Himalayan mountains and surrounding valleys, followed by Durpin Dara Hill for spectacular views of Kalimpong and the Teesta River. Explore Zang Dhok Palri Phodang Monastery and Pine View Nursery. Later, drive towards Darjeeling through scenic mountain roads. Check in at the hotel and spend the evening at leisure. Overnight stay in Darjeeling.

    Deolo HillDurpin Dara HillZang Dhok Palri Phodang MonasteryPine View NurseryScenic PhotographyKalimpong–Darjeeling DriveTea Garden ViewsDarjeeling Hotel Stay

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  3. Day 3

    Darjeeling Full-Day Sightseeing

    Start the day early with an unforgettable visit to Tiger Hill to witness the beautiful sunrise over Mount Kanchenjunga. On the way back, visit Ghoom Monastery and Batasia Loop. After breakfast, continue Darjeeling sightseeing with visits to the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute, Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park, Tenzing Rock, Tea Garden, Tibetan Refugee Self-Help Centre and other popular attractions. In the evening, enjoy a relaxing walk around Darjeeling Mall Road / Chowrasta. Overnight stay in Darjeeling.

    Tiger Hill SunriseKanchenjunga ViewsGhoom MonasteryBatasia LoopTea GardenHimalayan Mountaineering InstituteHimalayan Zoological ParkTenzing RockChowrasta & Mall Road

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  4. Day 4

    Darjeeling – Kurseong Transfer & Sightseeing

    After breakfast, check out from Darjeeling and proceed towards the peaceful hill town of Kurseong. Enjoy the beautiful tea gardens, misty mountain scenery and colonial charm along the route. On arrival, visit attractions such as Eagle’s Craig, Dow Hill, Forest Museum, Makaibari Tea Estate and Kurseong viewpoints. Spend the evening enjoying the calm atmosphere of the hills. Overnight stay in Kurseong.

    Kurseong – NJP/IXB Departure

    After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed towards NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport (IXB) for your onward journey. Enjoy the final views of the Himalayan foothills as your memorable Kalimpong–Darjeeling–Kurseong tour comes to an end.
