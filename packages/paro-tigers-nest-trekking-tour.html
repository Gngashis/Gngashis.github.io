Overview

Experience the enchanting beauty of Bhutan on a memorable 4 Nights & 5 Days journey covering the picturesque valleys of Paro and Thimphu. Explore Bhutan’s rich culture, ancient monasteries, majestic dzongs and breathtaking Himalayan landscapes. Visit iconic attractions such as Tiger’s Nest Monastery, Paro Rinpung Dzong, Kyichu Lhakhang, Buddha Dordenma, Memorial Chorten and Tashichho Dzong. This relaxing Himalayan getaway offers a perfect blend of nature, spirituality, culture and scenic beauty, making it ideal for families, couples and leisure travellers.

Day by day

The itinerary

  1. Day 1

    IXB – PHUENTSHOLING/JAIGAON TRANSFER

    Arrive at Bagdogra Airport (IXB) and meet your representative before proceeding towards Phuentsholing, the gateway to Bhutan. Enjoy the scenic journey through the lush landscapes of North Bengal. On arrival, complete the required immigration and entry formalities, check in to your hotel and relax. In the evening, you may explore the local market and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of this border town

    IXB Pickup | Scenic Drive | Immigration & Entry Formalities | Phuentsholing | Local Market

    DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  2. Day 2

    PHUENTSHOLING/JAIGAON – THIMPHU TRANSFER & SIGHTSEEING

    After breakfast, proceed towards Thimphu, the charming capital of Bhutan. Enjoy a scenic drive through beautiful Himalayan valleys, forests and mountain roads. On the way, experience the spectacular Chukha Valley and continue towards Thimphu. After arrival and hotel check-in, visit Memorial Chorten and explore the vibrant streets and local markets of Thimphu.

    Himalayan Scenic Drive | Chukha Valley | Memorial Chorten | Thimphu City | Local Market

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  3. Day 3

    THIMPHU – PARO TRANSFER & SIGHTSEEING

    After breakfast, check out and proceed to the beautiful Paro Valley. Before departure from Thimphu, visit the magnificent Buddha Dordenma Statue, offering panoramic views of the valley, followed by Tashichho Dzong, an impressive fortress and important administrative centre. Continue to Paro and visit Paro Rinpung Dzong and the historic Kyichu Lhakhang. Check in to your hotel and enjoy a relaxing evening in Paro town

    Buddha Dordenma | Tashichho Dzong | Thimphu–Paro Drive | Rinpung Dzong | Kyichu Lhakhang | Paro Valley

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  4. Day 4

    PARO – TIGER'S NEST MONASTERY EXCURSION

    After an early breakfast, prepare for the highlight of your Bhutan journey—the Tiger's Nest Monastery (Taktsang). Enjoy the scenic hike through pine forests and mountain trails to reach this spectacular monastery perched dramatically on a cliff above the Paro Valley. Spend time exploring the monastery and enjoying breathtaking Himalayan views before hiking back to Paro. The evening is free for relaxation or exploring the charming streets and local shops of Paro.

    Tiger's Nest Hike | Taktsang Monastery | Pine Forest Trail | Himalayan Views | Photography | Paro Town

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  5. Day 5

    PARO – IXB DEPARTURE

    After breakfast, check out from the hotel and begin your return journey towards Phuentsholing and Bagdogra Airport (IXB) for your onward journey. Enjoy the beautiful Himalayan scenery along the way and conclude your memorable Bhutan holiday filled with cultural experiences, monasteries, valleys and mountain views.

    Paro Departure | Himalayan Scenic Drive | Border Formalities | IXB Drop | Last Bhutan Memories

    Dinner
