Overview

Experience the magical beauty of Bhutan on a memorable 5 Nights & 6 Days journey covering 1 Night in Phuentsholing, 2 Nights in Thimphu and 2 Nights in Paro. Explore Bhutan’s peaceful valleys, rich culture, ancient monasteries, majestic dzongs and breathtaking Himalayan landscapes. Highlights include Phuentsholing, Buddha Dordenma, Tashichho Dzong, Memorial Chorten, Paro Rinpung Dzong, Kyichu Lhakhang and the iconic Tiger’s Nest Monastery. This scenic Bhutan getaway offers a perfect blend of nature, spirituality, culture and Himalayan adventure.

Day by day

The itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Pickup from IXB/NJP to Phuentsholing

    Upon arrival at Bagdogra Airport (IXB), meet our representative and begin your scenic journey towards Phuentsholing, the gateway to Bhutan. Enjoy the beautiful drive through lush green landscapes, tea gardens and picturesque surroundings. On arrival, complete the necessary entry formalities and check in to the hotel. Spend the evening at leisure, relax and explore the local surroundings of Phuentsholing. Overnight stay in Phuentsholing/Jaigaon.

    Pickup from IXB/NJP Scenic Drive to Phuentsholing Tea Garden & Countryside Views Bhutan Entry Formalities Hotel Check-in & Leisure

    DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  2. Day 2

    Phuentsholing to Thimphu

    After breakfast, check out from the hotel, after completing all the immigration process & Permits. Proceed towards Thimphu, the capital city of Bhutan. Enjoy a scenic drive through winding mountain roads, lush valleys, forests and beautiful Himalayan landscapes. En route, experience the changing scenery as you gradually enter the heart of Bhutan. On arrival in Thimphu, check in to the hotel and relax. If time permits, enjoy a short evening walk around the city and explore the local market. Overnight stay in Thimphu.

    Immigration & Entry Formalities Permit Processing Scenic Drive to Thimphu Himalayan Mountain & Valley Views Hotel Check-in & Leisure

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  3. Day 3

    Thimphu Sightseeing

    After breakfast, proceed for a full-day sightseeing tour of Thimphu, exploring the cultural and scenic highlights of Bhutan’s capital city. Visit the magnificent Buddha Dordenma Statue, Memorial Chorten, Tashichho Dzong, Motithang Takin Preserve and Kuensel Phodrang. Enjoy the city’s peaceful atmosphere, traditional Bhutanese architecture and beautiful Himalayan surroundings. In the evening, explore the local market and enjoy some leisure time in Thimphu before returning to the hotel. Overnight stay in Thimphu.

    Buddha Dordenma Statue Memorial Chorten Tashichho Dzong Motithang Takin Preserve Kuensel Phodrang Local Market & City Walk

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  4. Day 4

    Thimphu to Punakha via Dochula Pass

    After breakfast, check out from the hotel and drive towards **Punakha**, enjoying a scenic journey through the beautiful Himalayan landscapes of Bhutan. En route, visit **Dochula Pass**, famous for its 108 Druk Wangyal Chortens and panoramic mountain views. Continue the drive through lush valleys and winding mountain roads to Punakha. On arrival, visit the magnificent **Punakha Dzong**, situated at the confluence of the Mo Chhu and Pho Chhu rivers. Later, check in to the hotel and relax amidst the peaceful surroundings of Punakha. Overnight stay in Punakha.

    Scenic Drive to Punakha Dochula Pass Visit 108 Druk Wangyal Chortens Himalayan Mountain Views Punakha Dzong Visit Hotel Check-in & Leisure

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  5. Day 5

    Punakha to Paro

    After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed towards Paro, enjoying a scenic drive through Bhutan’s beautiful mountain landscapes and valleys. En route, enjoy the breathtaking views from Dochula Pass and visit Simtokha Dzong, subject to route and time conditions. Continue towards Paro and, on arrival, visit the historic Paro Rinpung Dzong and explore the charming Paro town. Later, check in to the hotel and relax amidst the peaceful surroundings of the Paro Valley. Overnight stay in Paro.

    Scenic Drive to Paro Himalayan Mountain & Valley Views Paro Rinpung Dzong Visit Paro Town Exploration Hotel Check-in & Leisure

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  6. Day 6

    Paro (Hike to Tiger’s Nest)

    After breakfast, proceed for the highlight of your Bhutan journey—the famous Tiger’s Nest Monastery (Taktsang Monastery). Begin the scenic hike through beautiful pine forests and mountain trails, enjoying breathtaking views of the Paro Valley along the way. Reach the spectacular monastery perched dramatically on a cliff and spend time exploring this sacred and historic site. After completing the hike, descend back and return to Paro town. Evening is free for relaxation, shopping or exploring the local surroundings. Overnight stay in Paro.

    Tiger’s Nest Monastery Visit Hiking Through Pine Forests Mountain & Paro Valley Views Monastery Exploration Photography Return to Paro & Leisure

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  7. Day 7

    Paro to IXB/NJP Departure

    After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed from Paro to Bagdogra Airport (IXB) for your onward journey. Enjoy the scenic drive through Bhutan and the beautiful Himalayan landscapes along the way. On arrival at IXB, your memorable Bhutan tour comes to an end with wonderful memories of the journey.

    Hotel Check-out Scenic Drive to IXB Himalayan Landscape Views Airport Drop-off at Bagdogra Departure & Tour Completion

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
