Overview

Experience the peaceful beauty and unique culture of Bhutan on this wonderful 3 Nights & 4 Days Bhutan tour, covering 1 Night in Thimphu and 2 Nights in Paro. Begin your journey with a scenic drive to Thimphu, the charming capital city of Bhutan, surrounded by beautiful Himalayan landscapes. Explore the city’s cultural attractions, traditional architecture and local markets before continuing to Paro, one of Bhutan’s most picturesque valleys. Discover ancient monasteries, historic landmarks and stunning mountain scenery while enjoying Bhutan’s peaceful atmosphere and traditional way of life. The highlight of the trip is the famous Tiger’s Nest Monastery (Taktsang), dramatically located on a cliff above the Paro Valley. This short Bhutan getaway is perfect for families, couples and travellers looking for a refreshing combination of nature, culture, spirituality and Himalayan adventure.
