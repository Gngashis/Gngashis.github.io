Overview

Experience the magic of Gangtok, the capital of Sikkim, with this comprehensive tour package. Visit the iconic Rumtek Monastery, stroll along the vibrant MG Marg, and marvel at the pristine Tsomgo Lake surrounded by snow-capped peaks. This package offers a perfect blend of culture, spirituality, and natural beauty in the Eastern Himalayas.

  • Nathu La Pass

Day by day

The itinerary

  1. Day 1

    IXB/NJP – GANGTOK TRANSFER

    Arrive at Bagdogra Airport (IXB) / NJP Railway Station and proceed towards Gangtok, the beautiful capital of Sikkim. Enjoy the scenic journey through lush green forests, mountain valleys and the Teesta River. On arrival, check in to the hotel and relax. Evening is free to explore MG Marg, the vibrant heart of Gangtok, with its cafés, shops and pleasant mountain atmosphere.

    Airport/NJP Pickup | Scenic Himalayan Drive | Teesta River Views | Hotel Check-in | MG Marg

    DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  2. Day 2

    GANGTOK – TSOMGO LAKE, BABA MANDIR & NATHULA PASS EXCURSION

    After breakfast, proceed for an exciting full-day excursion to Tsomgo Lake, a spectacular high-altitude glacial lake surrounded by majestic mountains. Continue to Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir, a popular pilgrimage and tourist attraction. Later, proceed towards the historic Nathu La Pass, located on the Indo-China border, subject to permit and weather conditions. Return to Gangtok by evening and enjoy leisure time.

    : Nathu La Pass | Tsomgo Lake | Baba Mandir | Snow-Covered Mountains | Indo-China Border Views | Scenic Photography

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  3. Day 3

    GANGTOK FULL-DAY LOCAL SIGHTSEEING

    After breakfast, explore the major attractions of Gangtok. Visit Rumtek Monastery, Banjhakri Falls, Namgyal Institute of Tibetology, Do Drul Chorten, Flower Exhibition Centre and Tashi View Point. Experience Sikkim's beautiful Buddhist culture, waterfalls, monasteries and panoramic Himalayan scenery. Evening is free for shopping and leisure at MG Marg

    Rumtek Monastery | Banjhakri Falls | Tashi View Point | Flower Exhibition Centre | Do Drul Chorten | MG Marg

    Breakfast & DinnerDeluxe Hotel
  4. Day 4

    GANGTOK – IXB/NJP DEPARTURE

    After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed towards Bagdogra Airport (IXB) / NJP Railway Station for your onward journey. Enjoy the scenic mountain drive and the last beautiful views of Sikkim before your tour comes to an end.

    Gangtok Departure | Himalayan Views | Scenic Journey | IXB Airport Drop / NJP Railway Station Drop

    Dinner
