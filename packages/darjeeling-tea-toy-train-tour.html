Overview

Discover the timeless charm of Darjeeling, the Queen of the Himalayas. This tour takes you through lush tea gardens, offers a ride on the iconic Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (Toy Train), and treats you to breathtaking views of the Kanchenjunga from Tiger Hill. Visit the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute, explore the vibrant local markets, and immerse yourself in the rich colonial heritage of this legendary hill station.

  • Himalayan Mountaineering Institute

  • Batasia Loop
