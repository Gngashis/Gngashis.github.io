Overview

A 6-day Darjeeling–Sikkim tour can begin with 2 nights in Darjeeling, covering Tiger Hill, Batasia Loop, Ghoom Monastery and local sightseeing. On Day 3, proceed to Pelling for scenic views and monastery visits, followed by a transfer to Gangtok on Day 4. Spend Day 5 exploring Gangtok, including Tsomgo Lake and Baba Mandir, with Nathula Pass subject to permit and weather conditions. On Day 6, depart from Gangtok for Bagdogra Airport or NJP Railway Station.

Journey highlights

  • Tiger Hill Sunrise • Darjeeling Tea Gardens • Batasia Loop • Pelling • Himalayan Views • Gangtok • MG Marg • Tsomgo Lake • Baba Mandir • Nathula Pass

Day by day

The itinerary

  1. Day 1

    : Arrival at IXB/NJP – Darjeeling

    Arrive at Bagdogra Airport (IXB) / New Jalpaiguri Railway Station (NJP) and proceed towards Darjeeling, the beautiful Queen of the Hills. Enjoy the scenic mountain drive through winding roads, tea gardens and Himalayan landscapes. On arrival, check in to the hotel and relax. In the evening, explore Mall Road and Chowrasta.
