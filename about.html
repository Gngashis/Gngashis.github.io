The house

Travel, arranged

MyTravelExpert is a small travel house devoted to the Eastern Himalaya — the tea hills of Darjeeling, the monasteries of Sikkim, the river plains of the Dooars and the fortress valleys of Bhutan. We keep a deliberately short collection: sixteen journeys we have walked, priced honestly, and refined season after season.

When you write to us, a travel expert — not a bot, not a call centre — reads your note, calls you back, and shapes the journey around you. Permits, pacing, rooms and viewpoints are all confirmed before you commit to anything.
